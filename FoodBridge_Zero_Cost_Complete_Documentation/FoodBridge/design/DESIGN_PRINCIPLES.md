# Design Principles

1. Operational clarity: show what needs action now.
2. Trust through evidence: distinguish verified, pending, donor-declared and estimated.
3. Progressive disclosure: reveal sensitive detail only when authorised.
4. Consistency without monotony: shared patterns, role-specific information architecture.
5. Accessibility by default: semantic HTML, keyboard, visible focus and contrast.
6. Honest interface: no fake counts, simulated live maps or fabricated testimonials.
7. Low-friction forms: grouped fields, explicit units and inline errors.
8. Calm urgency: visible deadlines without alarmist animation.
9. Responsive first: core workflows on narrow phones and unreliable networks.
10. Human control: recommendations remain advisory.
