# User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

- As a donor, I publish surplus with an expiry. **Accept:** required fields, units and times validate; listing is created under my approved organisation.
- As a recipient, I browse suitable nearby food. **Accept:** only approved and route-eligible listings appear; distance is labelled straight-line.
- As a recipient, I claim a listing. **Accept:** transaction rechecks deadline/eligibility and exactly one claim wins.
- As a donor and recipient, we confirm handover. **Accept:** both parties confirm; actual quantity/unit and timestamps are recorded.
- As an administrator, I verify an organisation. **Accept:** decision, reviewer, time and reason are recorded; verification documents remain offline.
- As a mess operator, I record leftovers. **Accept:** prepared, served and leftover quantities have explicit units and cannot be negative.
- As a project owner, I need no-cost operation. **Accept:** Firebase stays Spark; no billing account, paid API, paid dependency or forbidden endpoint exists.
- As a user, I receive updates. **Accept:** in-app inbox updates while online and unread records appear on next sign-in; no background delivery is promised.
