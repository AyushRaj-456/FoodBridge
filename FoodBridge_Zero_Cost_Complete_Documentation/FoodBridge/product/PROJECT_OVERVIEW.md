# Project Overview

## Problem
Institutional kitchens and community food providers generate time-sensitive surplus while nearby organisations struggle to discover it. Food recovery is fragmented and often poorly measured. Material that cannot be redistributed to people may have another responsible organic-waste pathway, but categories must never be conflated.

## Solution
FoodBridge connects four stages: **Prevent** overproduction through mess data; **Redistribute** suitable surplus through verified recipient matching; **Recycle** compatible organic material through approved facilities; **Measure** actual outcomes with traceable evidence.

## Users
Donor staff; verified NGOs; later, approved cattle farms and compost/biogas operators; mess analysts; platform administrators.

## Pilot
One college-hostel mess, a small manually verified recipient cohort, direct collection only, one or two weeks. Begin with baseline measurement, then enable limited live listings only after institutional and food-safety approval.

## Out of scope for MVP
Delivery fleet, payments, public social network, blockchain, AI freshness certification, automated legal verification, automatic animal-feed approval, complex ML and enterprise billing.

## Success
Lower measured surplus per person served where comparable; faster listing-to-collection; complete handover records; no unresolved critical safety/privacy incident; credible evidence for every impact metric.
