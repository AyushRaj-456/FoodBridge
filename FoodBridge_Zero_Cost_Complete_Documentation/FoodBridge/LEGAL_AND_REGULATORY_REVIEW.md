# Legal and Regulatory Review Register

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Obtain qualified Indian legal and food-safety review before live operation.

## Food safety
Review the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 and applicable regulations, particularly the Recovery and Distribution of Surplus Food Regulation, 2019; Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses Regulation, 2011; and relevant hygiene, storage, transport, labelling and recordkeeping rules. Official FSSAI regulations index: https://www.fssai.gov.in/food-law/regulations. Confirm current notified text and amendments; original notifications prevail over compilations.

## Personal data
Review the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025, including phased commencement and duties applicable on launch date. Official sources: India Code https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/22037?view_type=browse and MeitY https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa.

Also review organisation-document verification, liability allocation, beneficiary photography/consent, privacy notice, grievance, retention, local solid-waste/compost rules, animal-feed/veterinary rules and delivery insurance if delivery is later introduced.

No disclaimer or terms page substitutes for compliance, competent review, safe operating procedures or institutional approval.
