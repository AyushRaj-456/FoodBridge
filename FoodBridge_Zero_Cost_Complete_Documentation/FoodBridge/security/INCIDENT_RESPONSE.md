# Incident Response

Sequence: detect and record; assign incident lead; preserve relevant audit evidence; contain affected account, listing or route; assess affected people/data; notify institutional owners and authorities where required; communicate factually; recover and validate; conduct post-incident review. For suspected food-safety incident, pause the affected route/listings immediately and follow the institution's approved SOP. Do not collect or upload extra personal documents as an ad hoc response. Record timeline, decisions, impact, corrective action and owner.
