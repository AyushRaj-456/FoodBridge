# Data Privacy

Apply purpose limitation, minimisation, transparency, accuracy, storage limitation, safeguards and accountability. Collect only account, organisation, listing, pickup, handover and mess data required for the pilot. Do not collect identity-document scans, beneficiary images, passwords, OTPs or unnecessary personal data. Exact pickup location is restricted until claim. Provide a clear privacy notice, correction path, retention schedule and grievance contact. Confirm legal and institutional requirements before pilot. Do not intentionally collect children's personal data.
